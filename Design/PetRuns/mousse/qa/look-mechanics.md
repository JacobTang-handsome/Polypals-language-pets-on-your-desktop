# Mousse look mechanics

- Stable anchor: seated forepaws, hindquarters, and tail root stay registered on one baseline; do not rotate the entire cheetah.
- `000 up`: frontal chest; pupils, muzzle, brow, and a restrained neck lift point toward the screen top. Rounded ears remain symmetric enough to preserve identity.
- `090 screen-right`: head yaws toward viewer-right; nose and pupils cross right of head center, the screen-left cheek and near ear become more visible, while the far ear and far cheek partially occlude. The curled tail stays attached.
- `180 down`: frontal chest; chin tucks and eyes, muzzle, and eyelids point toward the screen bottom without reading as failure.
- `270 screen-left`: inverse of `090`; nose and pupils cross left of head center, with corresponding cheek and ear occlusion.
- Diagonals smoothly interpolate head, eye, muzzle, ear, and upper-body follow-through. Spot pattern may foreshorten but must remain recognizably continuous; cream muzzle/chest and dark tail tip remain stable.
