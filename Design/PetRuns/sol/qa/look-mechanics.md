# Sol look mechanics

- Stable anchor: both seated paws, hips, and tail base stay registered on one baseline; the full body never rotates as a rigid cutout.
- `000 up`: frontal torso; eyes, muzzle, brow, and a small neck extension point toward the screen top. Both ears remain recognizable.
- `090 screen-right`: head yaws toward the viewer's right; nose and both pupils move right of head center, the left cheek becomes more visible, and the far ear is partly occluded. Tail remains attached and follows only subtly.
- `180 down`: frontal torso; chin tucks, upper eyelids lower, and eyes and muzzle point toward the screen bottom without becoming a failed/sad pose.
- `270 screen-left`: inverse of `090`; nose and pupils move left of head center, right cheek is more visible, and the far ear is partly occluded.
- Diagonals interpolate eye, muzzle, ear, and neck movement continuously. Orange forehead blaze, cream muzzle/chest, black eye construction, paws, and white-tipped tail must not change sides or detach.
